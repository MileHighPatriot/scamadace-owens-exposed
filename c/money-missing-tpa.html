1 Her claim — what she said

In July 2026 Owens posed pointed public questions about whether Charlie was concerned about money missing from Turning Point Action during his last weeks. The question format invites the audience to fill in a murder motive: someone stole, Charlie noticed, Charlie died.

Nonprofit and PAC finance disputes, if they exist, are serious governance issues. They are not automatically homicide motives, and they do not appear in the public charging case as the theory of the UVU shooting. Suspicion-by-question about ledgers is not a substitute for a forensic case against a named financial plotter.
