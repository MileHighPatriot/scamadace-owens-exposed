1 Her claim — what she said

Across months of content, Owens has often refused a clean lone-actor baseline. Even after arrest, charging, and a preliminary hearing, the posture frequently becomes: maybe he was there, but he is a patsy/tool; something much bigger — foreign services, TPUSA betrayal, federal theater — remains.

This is an unfalsifiable escape hatch. Every new exhibit against Robinson can be reabsorbed as “part of the frame” or “he was used.” A serious bigger-plot claim needs affirmative evidence of handlers, money, communications, or co-conspirators — not a permanent maybe.
