1 Her claim — what she said

Owens packaged a list of “verifiable lies” she said Turning Point USA spread after Kirk’s murder — including stories about Mikey McCoy’s appearance and calls, claims about police directing camera removal, and soft-selling of staff as heroes. She used the list to argue the official assassination story is compromised.

Some first-day organizational statements after a livestreamed murder will be wrong, incomplete, or PR-shaped. That is a known failure mode of crisis communications. It is not automatically a confession that Israel, Egypt, France, or TPUSA executives ran the hit.

Misleading leap: (A) “TPUSA botched or spun X detail” becomes (B) “therefore Robinson is a patsy and the real conspiracy is huge.” (A) can be partly true while (B) remains unsupported.

Evidence 1
Tier 5 — Analysis / logic (clearly labeled)

PR failure ≠ forensic collapse of the rifle case

Even if several TPUSA talking points were inaccurate, the public case still includes multi-angle courtyard video, a recovered rifle, DNA statistics reported in court, and confessional communications described by prosecutors. Organizational spin does not delete those exhibits.

Audit each alleged lie on its own: was it false, who said it, does it touch mechanism/identity? Most of Owens’s list items are personnel optics, not ballistics.

Evidence 2
Tier 5 — Analysis / logic (clearly labeled)

List-making creates a false sense of cumulative proof

Ten soft disputes feel like a dossier. Quantity of grievances is not quality of homicide evidence. Force item-by-item relevance: which lie, if true, would move the bullet from a rooftop rifle to a foreign cell?
