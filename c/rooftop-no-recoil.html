4K Losee rooftop footage shows “no recoil,” a military lateral crawl, and a flash — so it cannot be amateur Tyler Robinson firing a Mauser

1 Her claim — what she said

On July 28–29, 2026 (Candace Ep 367), Candace Owens promoted and aired what she called “never before seen” zoomed Losee Center rooftop footage of the alleged gunman. On X she teased: “Never before seen footage from the Losee Rooftop. Did officials already perjure themselves?” and that the episode “Might even free a young man who is being wrongly framed.”

On the show she argued frame-by-frame that the figure performs a military “lateral crawl,” that a flash appears during the crawl, that something is “flung forward” (which her brain read as unfolding a gun), and — crucially — that viewers cannot observe the strong shoulder kickback expected from a German Mauser 98-class rifle. She tied this to an earlier exclusive witness who allegedly said he saw no recoil and that the person looked professionally trained. She concluded the sequence “doesn’t feel” like Tyler Robinson.

This is a classic re-litigation of identity and mechanism by consumer video interpretation. Court packages already placed a rifle case, DNA work, and rooftop surveillance before the public. Absence of a clearly visible kickback on heavily zoomed, distant, compressed video is not ballistics. Professionals and amateurs both crawl; “looks tactical” is not a positive ID of a military hit team. A flash on a rooftop can be a phone, reflection, lens artifact, or clothing — not proof of a signal mirror for a multi-operator plot.

Evidence 1
Tier 1 — Court / charging / official case facts

Court-facing case remains a rooftop rifle homicide, not a “no shot occurred” file

July 2026 preliminary hearing coverage and wire reporting walked through surveillance placing a figure on the Losee-area rooftop geometry, a recovered rifle, DNA analysis, and confessional-style communications. Owens’s consumer zoom of the same class of rooftop media does not erase that package.

If her claim is “no rifle was fired,” she is reopening the mic-bomb / alternate-mechanism war already disproved by multi-angle courtyard gunshot media. If her claim is only “that blur isn’t Robinson,” she still needs affirmative identity of a different shooter — not vibes about crawl technique.

Evidence 2
Tier 3 — Primary video / audio of the event

No visible recoil on zoomed CCTV is not ballistics

Recoil visibility depends on camera angle, distance, compression, frame rate, zoom crop, body position, bipod/bag support, and whether the exact fire frame is even in the clip. Owens herself apologized that the video is not “Benny Johnson 4K” clarity and that timestamps were pushed.

A Mauser-class bolt gun has real recoil. That does not mean every phone or campus camera will render a Hollywood shoulder slam. Expert reconstruction uses high-frame-rate synced video, muzzle report timing, and recovered weapon testing — not audience chat polls (“Anybody see a recoil?”).

Sources

  • CBS: shooting timeline / scene basics
Evidence 3
Tier 4 — Multi-outlet reporting citing documents

“Lateral crawl = professional hitman” is pattern-seeking, not identification

Military manuals describe common low crawls used by hunters, airsofters, and anyone trying not to silhouette on a roof. Performing a low crawl does not identify a unit, a foreign service, or exclude a 23-year-old who planned a murder.

Owens pairs “looks trained” with “not giving Tyler Robinson” without a validated height/gait/face comparison that survives court methods. Family recognition disputes and blurry stairwell stills are already catalogued elsewhere; Ep 367 does not close identity with crawl cosplay.

Evidence 4
Tier 5 — Analysis / logic (clearly labeled)

Flash-during-crawl speculation is unfalsifiable content fuel

She lists possibilities (signal mirror, flag, background car, lens weirdness) then invites the audience to treat mystery as proof of coordination. That is not investigation. A real signal-mirror claim needs a second camera angle, light physics, or recovered device — not “we’re just going to show you that.”

Evidence 5
Tier 5 — Analysis / logic (clearly labeled)

Promo language (“free a young man”) is advocacy, not evidence

The July 28 X promo framed the episode as potentially freeing a wrongly framed defendant and shaming “Fed slop” sellers. That is campaign messaging. Evidence either matches Robinson to the roof shot or it doesn’t. Marketing copy about freeing him is not a third ballistics test.
