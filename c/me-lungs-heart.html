On July 29, 2026 Owens posted a show rundown: what happened to Charlie’s body after it left Utah; Tyler Robinson’s defense filing regarding the medical examiner’s report citing traumatic injuries to Charlie’s lungs and heart; and another Blake Neff “moved goalpost.”

High-velocity rifle trauma to the neck can involve massive hemorrhage, temporary cavity effects, and complex damage pathways that lay audiences summarize poorly.
