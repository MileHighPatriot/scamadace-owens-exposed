1 Her claim — what she said

Reporting on Owens’s shows notes a quip that if roles were reversed — Erika killed, Charlie behaving as Erika has in leadership/public life after the murder — Charlie would be on trial for his wife’s murder. The line is designed to plant suspicion without producing evidence of Erika’s participation in the UVU shot.
