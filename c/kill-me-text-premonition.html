1 Her claim — what she said

Owens has highlighted texts and warnings in which Kirk expressed fear he might be killed — including packaging around a message to the effect that “they” were going to kill him, alongside 2018 prophecy-style dread texts. Fear can be authentic. “They” is not a cast list.

The misleading move is smuggling Egypt/France/Israel/TPUSA-executive plots into a pronoun. Premonition and threat-environment anxiety are common for high-profile political speakers. They do not identify the person who later fired a rifle from a rooftop.
