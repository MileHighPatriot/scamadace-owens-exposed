1 Her claim — what she said

During Ep 367’s rooftop segment, Owens argued the official narrative has Robinson in skinny jeans with a phone in a pocket, yet somehow carrying a minimum ~3'6" fully assembled Mauser-class rifle as if “Crip Walking” it onto the roof. She asked whether he wiggled it out of a tight ankle cuff or dropped his pants on the roof, treating the packaging as absurd on its face.

This is misleading because charging stories about long guns typically involve how a weapon was transported (broken down, bag, case, loose carry under clothing, pre-staged) — details that evolve in discovery. Mocking a straw-man “rifle fully assembled inside skinny jeans leg” is easy comedy; it is not a proof that no rifle reached the roof. Roof video of a figure with a long object and a recovered rifle still have to be answered on their own terms.
