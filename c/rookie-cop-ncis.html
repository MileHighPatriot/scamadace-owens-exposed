1 Her claim — what she said

In Candace Ep 367 (July 28–29, 2026), after arguing bomb dogs failed to find the rifle, Owens said federal direction sent “rookie” officers back into a field search and that Mitchell Curtis of Pleasant Grove Police — a 2025 UVU graduate — found the gun. She then spent the arc on his father, Foster Curtis: ~21 years at NCIS rising to division chief in the Office of Inspector General, then a June 2, 2025 move to Richmond, Virginia interim inspector general amid local controversy.

She explicitly connected NCIS’s role investigating major Navy/Marine crime to her hypothesis that the assassination “may have been plotted and planned and executed by our military,” making the father’s former office the “office that would be in charge of that investigation.” The rhetorical payload: the gun find is “beginner’s luck” engineered by a Fed family tree, not ordinary policing.

This is guilt-by-association genealogy. Having a parent who once worked federal law enforcement does not prove a son planted a rifle. Local officers find evidence in large scenes every day. NCIS jurisdiction over Navy crimes does not make every NCIS alumnus’s child a co-conspirator in a Utah political murder. She offers no document that Foster directed the UVU search, no order to plant, and no chain-of-custody break tied to the father.

Evidence 1
Tier 5 — Analysis / logic (clearly labeled)

Association is not participation

Conspiracy content thrives on “tree of brand new faces.” A young local officer’s parent having a federal résumé is a coincidence class that exists in every large metro. To convert coincidence into planting, you need orders, access logs, tampered seals, or confession — none of which Ep 367 produces.

Evidence 2
Tier 1 — Court / charging / official case facts

The state’s exhibit is still the rifle + DNA + scene work, not the biography of the finder

Court presentations emphasize scientific and video links between the recovered weapon and Robinson. Impeaching the first finder would require showing the rifle was introduced from elsewhere with broken custody. Owens instead narrates “luck” and NCIS prestige TV lore.

Evidence 3
Tier 5 — Analysis / logic (clearly labeled)

Jurisdiction sleight of hand

She says if the military plotted the kill, NCIS would investigate Navy/Marine crime — then treats a retired/transferred NCIS father’s son finding a gun in Utah as if that completes a military-plot proof. NCIS does not own all U.S. political murder investigations. The leap from “dad worked NCIS” to “military assassination covered by NCIS alumni” is fan fiction structure.

Evidence 4
Tier 4 — Multi-outlet reporting citing documents

Career moves, local IG controversies, and NDAs happen in government without being pre-positioning for a UVU hit three months later. Absent authenticated tasking connecting Foster Curtis to Utah ops on Sept 9–11, the timeline is decorative.
