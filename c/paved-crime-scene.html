1 Her claim — what she said

A recurring cover-up motif holds that the scene was cleaned, restricted, or “paved” so the truth cannot be known. Owens-adjacent content pairs this with skepticism of federal/state handling.

Misleading: confusing “the public cannot walk the roof forever” with “the case file is empty.”
