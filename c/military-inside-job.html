Evidence 1
Tier 5 — Analysis / logic (clearly labeled)

Anonymous military tips are suspense, not proof

“A guy in the military told me” cannot be audited by the audience. No rank, no document, no channel. It is a storytelling device that borrows institutional authority without institutional accountability.

Anonymous authority is optimized for virality and minimized for accountability. The audience cannot examine incentives, access, or existence. That is acceptable as a lead-generation tool in journalism only when it is later nailed down with documents or on-record confirmation. It is unacceptable as the final form of a claim that a president ordered a murder or that a military tip “blows the case open.”

A practical response to followers: “Great — when the source goes on record or the order is published, the claim upgrades. Until then it is an anecdote with a costume.” Refuse to treat costume as chain of custody.

Evidence 2
Tier 5 — Analysis / logic (clearly labeled)

Military-affiliated campus spaces prove adjacency, not authorship

Campuses host ROTC, recruiters, and veterans. Adjacency is not a tasking order for murder.

Inside-job language expands by treating every uniform or federal building within driving distance as a node in a plot. That method never runs out of nodes. Require named officers, communications, or authenticated operational product — not a map of ordinary military-civil infrastructure.

Evidence 3
Tier 5 — Analysis / logic (clearly labeled)

The Fort Huachuca human anchor failed a logistics check

The most concrete military-meeting narrative in her package depended on Harpole’s presence. Reported travel records place him in Dallas that day. When the named attendee fails the itinerary test, the “final coordination meeting” atmosphere loses its best prop.

Logistics are some of the best disproof tools available to non-experts because they are hard to spin. A body cannot be in a secret Arizona meeting and on a Texas itinerary at the same time without a further conspiracy to fake airline systems. Each added fake multiplies the claim’s cost. Occam’s razor is not vibes; it is cost accounting for assumptions.

How “inside job” language expands without evidence
