1 Her claim — what she said

On Ep 367 Owens asked why, during the manhunt, authorities detained Zachariah Koreshi and then released him with “no explanation,” while highlighting alleged family military ties and a military app connected to operations/Fort Huachuca/DoD money. She framed it as a “big mistake” the public deserves answers for — implying the real plot trail was dropped.

Early manhunts detain wrong people. Release after interview is normal when evidence does not hold. Family military service is common in Utah. Without charging documents, body-worn camera proof of a framed release, or evidence tying Koreshi to the roof shot, this is leftover POI folklore — adjacent to her broader Fort Huachuca / military-inside-job cluster already on this site.
