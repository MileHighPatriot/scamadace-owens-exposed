1 Her claim — what she said

Ep 367 opens with Owens attacking Andrew Kolvet as a serial liar about a White House meeting she says was on the books for Kolvet, Marina Miles, Justin Strife, and in-house counsel, allegedly near Netanyahu’s presence in town — then “suddenly canceled,” after which Kolvet denied a meeting. She says a White House employee source confirmed it, and that coincidence “begins to look and feel like a conspiracy when Andrew continues to lie so brazenly about everything.”

Even if a meeting was scheduled and scrubbed, that is organizational politics or security scheduling — not evidence Kolvet murdered Charlie Kirk or covered up an Israeli hit. Owens’s method is to treat every TPUSA calendar discrepancy as bloodstain evidence. Without authenticated visitor logs, she has an anonymous source war, not a proven Netanyahu coordination plot.
