1 Her claim — what she said

In November 2025, Candace Owens published an “URGENT” statement claiming that French President Emmanuel Macron and Brigitte Macron had ordered and paid for her assassination. She said a high-ranking French government employee contacted her, that the green light went to a small team in France’s National Gendarmerie Intervention Group, that one Israeli was on the squad, and that the plans were formalized. She linked the allegation to her broader claims about French involvement around Charlie Kirk’s death and to her ongoing war with the Macrons over her false claims about Brigitte Macron’s sex and identity.

This is not a UVU ballistics claim. It is included because Owens braided it into the same post-September 2025 narrative universe: France, Israel, hit squads, and her own centrality to the story of Kirk’s murder. It is also one of the clearest examples of a maximal allegation offered with minimal proof.

Evidence 1
Tier 4 — Multi-outlet reporting citing documents

No public proof package accompanied a presidential murder-for-hire claim

Owens said the Macrons paid for her assassination, citing an unnamed high-ranking French official, a GIGN-linked team, and an Israeli member, with plans “formalized.” Major outlets reported the allegation and the void: no documents, no named officers, no intercepts, no arrested squad.

For an accusation against a head of state, the void is decisive. Viral reach is not corroboration. Forty million views cannot replace one authentic order.

Foreign-force claims are easy to stage because audiences cannot easily check deployment records. That asymmetry is why the proof bar should go up, not down. Presence of a foreign military unit at a U.S. political murder would be one of the most documented events of the decade if real. A podcast assertion is not how such events surface.

Press for the minimum viable proof: a named unit, a named operator, a date-stamped location record, or authenticated imagery of identified personnel performing the act. Color symbolism, freemason news, and personal beef with French politicians do not meet the minimum.

Anonymous high officials are the cheapest possible proof
