Evidence 1
Tier 5 — Analysis / logic (clearly labeled)

Authentication is a lab problem

Donors saying “fake” is a tip. Authentication needs file provenance, acoustic/forensic analysis, and chain of custody. Podcast tip lines are not spectrograms.

Side recordings from other events can be mislabeled, edited, or disputed for ordinary political reasons. Until a lab product or documentary provenance lands, treat “donors told me it’s fake” as an unverified lead — useful only if it later hardens into exhibits, useless as a pillar for UVU mechanism theories.

Evidence 2
Tier 5 — Analysis / logic (clearly labeled)

Side media fights are often strategic distractions

Disputes about non-UVU recordings can absorb hours without moving the courtyard mechanism an inch. Ask whether the tape is load-bearing for the murder theory. If not, park it.

The assassination case turns on the Utah courtyard shot, the wound, the weapon class, and the charged defendant. An Aspen tape dispute may matter for media integrity in isolation; it does not, by itself, prove a mic bomb, a foreign hit team, or that Robinson is a patsy.

Evidence 3
Tier 5 — Analysis / logic (clearly labeled)

Even a proven fake side tape has limited implications

People fake or misattribute media constantly in political wars. That is bad. It is not automatically proof of the mic bomb or foreign hit at UVU.

Media fraud elsewhere is a separate offense class from the forensics of Sept 10, 2025. Keep the scopes separate. If someone proves a side tape is fake, update the media ledger — then return to multi-angle courtyard evidence and the charging package. Do not launder a tape fight into a total rewrite of the murder mechanism.

Evidence 4
Tier 5 — Analysis / logic (clearly labeled)

How to keep focus

UVU shot first. Side tape second. Never reverse.

When a conversation about Kirk’s assassination drifts into Aspen audio authenticity, re-pin: what does this tape change about the gunshot, the rifle exhibits, or identity? If the honest answer is “nothing yet,” you are watching content expansion, not casework.
