Why this matters on an evidence site

A false narrative that keeps audience attention is also a business line. Multi-million-view “emergency” episodes, SUV photo teases, and daily courtroom spin are not free to produce — and they are not free of economic reward. You do not need a precise P&L to see the structure: claim → outrage/curiosity → views → ads/memberships → next claim. That incentive structure helps explain volume and refusal to land on the public rifle case.

This page does not claim money alone proves a claim false. It shows money is real. For the claims themselves, use the claim catalog and public record.

Methodology (transparent on purpose)

That uncertainty is why we publish a range, not a single theatrical number. Corrections with primary documents welcome via @America1st5280 · Corrections policy.

Bottom line (central case)

On transparent assumptions, Kirk-assassination content alone is consistent with roughly ~$0.9M (conservative) → ~$4–5M (central) → up to ~$10–13M (aggressive) in combined creator-side revenue over ~10.5 months (YouTube + sponsors + attributed memberships + soft other). That is enough to show a serious business line — and still far below viral $59M fantasy clips or $800K/week YouTube myths.
