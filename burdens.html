Burden-of-proof checklists

If a claim type cannot meet its checklist, it is not “suppressed truth” — it is unfinished fanfic.

Device / mic bomb

Must produce:

  • Autopsy language of blast injury
  • Residue / energetics lab
  • Device reconstruction of unit on body that day
  • Mechanism that replaces multi-angle gunshot media

Exploding / rigged microphone · iPhone gun / phone-weapon theory · “Who mic’d Charlie” plot

Foreign hit team

Must produce:

  • Named service/unit
  • Authenticated presence records
  • Communications/money trail
  • Why local rifle case exists as perfect cover

Israel / Mossad killed Kirk · French Foreign Legion on the ground · Egyptian planes & hotel Egyptians · Military / Pentagon inside job

Inside job (TPUSA/staff)

Must produce:

  • Pre-crime knowledge communications
  • Actus reus (who did what on roof/stage)
  • Not merely PR errors or trauma behavior

TPUSA / inner-circle betrayal · Theories implicating Erika Kirk · Mikey McCoy suspicion arc

Doctored texts / planted gun

Must produce:

  • Forensic extraction hashes
  • Provenance break with named actors
  • Lab proof of non-fire if alleging prop rifle

Twiggs “Fed asset” / fake confession texts · Feds altered evidence / cover-up · Gun “not fired” / dust-in-barrel · Rookie cop / NCIS father “plant”

Foreknowledge

Must produce:

  • Timestamped communication before the shot
  • Not post-shot status updates

Shapiro “knew too early” · Shapiro security phone chain
