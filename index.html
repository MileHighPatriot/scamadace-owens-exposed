What the public record actually shows

  • When / where: Sept 10, 2025, outdoor TPUSA event at Utah Valley University, Orem, Utah. Kirk was speaking when a single shot struck him in the neck.
  • Who is charged: Tyler Robinson (22 at arrest; 23 at July 2026 hearing age reporting) of Utah — aggravated murder and related charges; prosecutors sought the death penalty.
  • Evidence package: DNA statistics on the recovered rifle strongly associating Robinson; pre-crime note; alleged confession texts + roommate remorse account; campus/rooftop surveillance at the shot window (presented in July 2026 preliminary hearing).
  • Mechanism in the official case: Bolt-action rifle shot from a distant rooftop (Losee Center) — not an exploding microphone, not a trap-door tunnel shot, not a charged foreign intelligence cell.
  • July 2026 status: Five-day preliminary hearing closed; final arguments set for early September; probable-cause ruling pending. The open-court case remains a firearms/identity case — not a mic-bomb case.
