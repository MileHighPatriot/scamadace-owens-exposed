How we rank evidence

Every disproof item on this site carries a tier badge. Higher tiers are harder evidence. Candace Owens’s show confidence is never treated as Tier 1. Analysis is useful — and always labeled as analysis.

Tier 1

Court filings, charging presentations, and case exhibits described in open proceedings

What a prosecutor puts on the record in charging documents or walks through in preliminary hearings — especially physical exhibits (weapon class, ballistic discussion, DNA on a specific part of a firearm), authenticated communications offered as evidence, and other materials subject to adversarial testing. We use Tier 1 only when it directly collides with the claim. We do not paste the whole case onto every page as filler.

Tier 2

Named officials and on-the-record law-enforcement / government briefings

Statements attributable to named officials, agencies, or formal press briefings about scene geometry, timeline, wound description, manhunt facts, or investigative posture. Weaker than a court exhibit, stronger than pundit commentary because it is institutional and on the record.

Tier 3

Primary video and audio of the event

Multi-angle phone and media recordings of the courtyard speech, the shot, and the immediate aftermath. Primary media is often the fastest disproof of mechanism fantasies because the public can pause the same frames.

Tier 4

Multi-outlet reporting that documents documents, data errors, or primary posts

Careful reporting that quotes charging presentations, reproduces Owens’s primary posts, or documents checkable errors (such as reading UTC as Utah local time on flight data). Secondary reporting is never as good as the underlying exhibit.

Tier 5

Analysis, logic, method critiques, and “how to pin the claim”

Explicit reasoning: unfalsifiable patterns, base rates, free-association, anonymous-source problems, and practical ways to force a claimant to produce a document or measurement. Tier 5 is useful — and always labeled so nobody confuses it with a lab report.

How to read a claim page: Her claim first → where she said it → then the evidence stack from hard collision facts down to method analysis. If two tiers conflict, prefer the higher tier. If something is still unknown, unknown is not proof of her theory.
