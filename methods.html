How the rhetoric works

Methods & playbook

Fact-checks alone lose to method. These are the recurring moves — with claim examples.

Archive hub Claim catalog Search Public record

Gish gallop

Flood the zone with dozens of anomalies so no single debunk “feels” sufficient. Counter: force one mechanism claim at a time with a kill criterion.

Examples: “Something much bigger” umbrella · “Pawn even if guilty” escape hatch · TPUSA “10 lies” cover-up

Motte-and-bailey

Bailey: “Israel/TPUSA killed him / mic bomb.” Motte: “I’m just asking questions / something feels off.” Counter: quote the bailey claim and demand its proof standard.

Examples: Israel / Mossad killed Kirk · Exploding / rigged microphone · Dream betrayal “evidence”

Dream-as-evidence

Treat dream content as investigative lead equal to documents. Counter: dreams are not admissible leads without waking-world corroboration.

Examples: Dream betrayal “evidence”

Guilt-by-association trees

Father worked NCIS → son planted gun; Jewish donor → Israeli hit. Counter: require tasking, access, and act — not résumés and ethnicity.

Examples: Rookie cop / NCIS father “plant” · Israel / Mossad killed Kirk · Ackman Hamptons “intervention”

Unfalsifiable “bigger plot”

Every contrary exhibit becomes “part of the frame.” Counter: write the minimum proof for handlers; if absent, the theory is a mood.

Examples: “Pawn even if guilty” escape hatch · “Something much bigger” umbrella · Robinson is innocent / patsy / Fed slop

Suspicion-by-question

“Why do I get the sense he was cremated?” shifts burden to targets to prove negatives. Counter: private facts aren’t public confessions.

Examples: Quietly cremated / burial secrecy · Missing TPUSA/TPA money motive · Neff hotel memory “gotcha”

Mechanism rotation without convergence

Tunnels → foreign teams → mic bomb → no recoil pro. Abandoned without kill criteria. Counter: timeline the pivots (see Pivots page).

Examples: Trap door & underground tunnels · Exploding / rigged microphone · Rooftop “no recoil” / military crawl · iPhone gun / phone-weapon theory

Celebrity authority laundering

A SEAL “feels” the shirt moved wrong becomes substitute for autopsy language. Counter: expertise must match the claim domain.

Examples: O’Neill / SEAL “Feds lying” boost

Procedure-as-conspiracy

Prelim hearing limits, dog non-alerts, or imperfect phone photos become completed proof of framing. Counter: discovery fights ≠ forgery.

Examples: Twiggs “Fed asset” / fake confession texts · Dogs missed the rifle / “bad sniffer” · Feds altered evidence / cover-up

Honor → capture arc

Start as “for Charlie,” end as permanent war on widow/staff while monetizing daily content. Counter: track who is attacked when mechanism claims fail.

Examples: Theories implicating Erika Kirk · TPUSA / inner-circle betrayal · Killed for Candace / “supposed to be you”

Pawn-even-if-guilty escape hatch

When identity evidence mounts, redefine success as “he didn’t act alone.” Counter: name the co-conspirators with evidence or drop it.

Examples: “Pawn even if guilty” escape hatch

Self-insert centrality

“It was supposed to be me,” French hit on Candace, finish-the-fight texts — keeps host at story center. Counter: victim is Kirk; host is not the crime scene.

Examples: Killed for Candace / “supposed to be you” · French / Macron hit on Candace · 2018 prophecy texts (context)
